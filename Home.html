Questions

Things people ask before they hire us.

Don't see yours? Call us and we will answer it in plain language.

Is the quote really fixed?

Yes. The price only changes if you ask for a change or we uncover hidden damage, such as rot behind a wall. In that case we stop, show you photos and get your approval in writing before doing any extra work.

Do you handle permits?

We pull every permit the job needs, schedule the inspections and are on site when the inspector arrives. Permit fees are listed as their own line in your quote.

How much is the deposit?

We take 10% to reserve your start date, then bill in stages as work is completed. Final payment is due only after your walkthrough list is done.

What does the guarantee cover?

Any defect in our workmanship for two years after completion. Manufacturer warranties on materials and appliances are passed directly to you.
